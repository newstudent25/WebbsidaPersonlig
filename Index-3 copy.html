About the project

The project Interactive Beats is an interactive installation that allows visitors to explore music creation in a hands-on way. By using a MIDI keyboard connected to a laptop, users can experiment with different sounds and melodies, shaping their own musical compositions. The setup is designed to be intuitive, making it accessible for people of all ages and skill levels. The project was showcased at various events, where it received positive feedback for its engaging and educational approach to music.
